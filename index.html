P.02About

"Motion is the difference between a message people scroll past and one they stop for."

I'm Karrar — a motion designer and AI creative working out of Baghdad, directing visuals for brands that want their advertising to actually get watched, not skipped.

My work sits between craft and computation: hand-built motion systems, AI-generated imagery, and an editor's instinct for pacing. Every project runs through the same discipline — concept, design, motion, delivery.
